Privacy Policy for L2DViewer

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Developer: CodePump

This Privacy Policy explains how L2DViewer ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Important: The App does NOT require an account, and we do NOT collect, store, or share any personal information.
  • No Account or Login: The App has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details.
  • No Ads or Analytics: The App does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics tools, or crash reporting services, and it does not use the Advertising ID.
  • No Access to Personal Files: The App only requests Internet access. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

2. Downloading Content

The App downloads the character list, thumbnails, and animation assets from a cloud storage service operated by a third party (Telegram). To do this, the App sends standard network requests that include your device's IP address, as with any internet connection. These requests do not include any personal information or identifiers from the App.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. Please review the Telegram Privacy Policy for details on how it handles network requests.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never uploaded:

  • Downloaded Assets: Animation files and thumbnails, so they can be viewed offline.
  • Preferences: Settings such as language, display options, recently viewed items, and the last version you have seen.

You can delete downloaded assets at any time from the App's Settings (Downloaded skins > Clear all), or remove all App data by uninstalling the App.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since the App does not collect personal information, no such data is stored by us.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
